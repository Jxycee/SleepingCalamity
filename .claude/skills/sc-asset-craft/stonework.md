# Stonework and modular runs: walls, fences, hedges, path edges

Part of **sc-asset-craft**: read SKILL.md first; this file adds what is specific to this kind of asset. Learned from the 2026-10-25 asset duo (blind test #8, a dry-stone wall). Numbers measured on one asset are marked as such: treat them as a starting point, not a rule.

- **Run axis and the preview.** The family generator `sc_millpond_walls.py` authors runs along +z. The preview's three-quarter view looks at +z, so a +z run previews end-on and its silhouette grade is meaningless: the shipped StoneWallTumbled's 2 was mostly that. Keep the family's +z convention so runs join, and preview it side-on with `sc_asset_preview.py --yaw 90`. Re-scored side-on, the shipped StoneWallTumbled only went from silhouette p26 to p34: its real problem is a near-flat top and low spill. Break the top line: stepped copes, a slumped section that drops, a stone standing proud.
- **Origin.** A run module's origin sits on the wall line at one end (or its centre), at y 0. That keeps modules joining every N m. **Skip `LK.to_base`** here: it centres on the bounding box, spill and saplings included, which pulls the origin off the line. State the origin in the header.
- **Stones:**
  - Use cheap chamfered stones, not sharp boxes. A plain `B.box` gives the paint pass's edge band nothing to bite on: a brush sweep from 0.35 to 0.8 only moved painterly from 8.6 to 9.3. Use **`HM.stone(size, chamfer=0.25, r, jit)`** (28 tris: chamfered top and bottom arrises, jittered corners, smooth normals). On the blind wall it took painterly from p46 to p74, value from p62 to p82 and form from 3 to 4, at about 2x the stone tris. A cheaper middle is `stone()` on the face stones and copes, with plain-box hearting.
  - Drop hidden faces with `HM.cull(geo, drop=('bottom', '-z'))`, which drops faces by facing, in Unity axes, and `HM.cull_inside(geo, boxes)`, which drops faces inside an occluder. That took the blind wall from 2 740 to 2 388 tris.
  - Course them: bigger stones low, copes on edge along the top, the face battered (0.72 m at the foot to 0.44 m at the top on a 1.1 m wall).
- **Confetti.** Many small stones each with its own tint push the scorer's fine/brush ratio over 0.35, which discounts painterly. Tint stones in patches of 4-8 neighbours (rule 6), not per stone.
- **Colour.** Field stone is grey with a cool shade side. The sunny face drifts warm, but not sandy yellow. Take the stone wall row of the materials table, with lichen as pale dabs and moss on the shaded face.
- **Value step.** Moss on the shaded face darkens that whole mass, which can drop value a grade at the 60th percentile. Brighten the copes to keep the spread.
- **Living parts on a prop** (a sapling in a wall, ivy, nettles):
  - **Leaves as mesh.** MillpondBuilder loads `_Cards` only for TreeKinds and BushKinds, so a prop's leaves must be mesh. Use small pinnate sprays (about 12 tris) around a dark core blob per tuft; flattened `H.blob` sprays read as palm fronds.
  - **Slot.** Put them on a seasonal slot: rank them with `sc_tint.py tint '<#hex>' --seasonal`, which prints every seasonal slot's spring, summer, autumn and winter colour for its tint.
  - **Winter.** A prop has no bare winter model, so its leaves stay all year. Keep them small, or on a slot that browns in autumn.
  - **Fruit.** The fruit rule (sc-foliage) still applies: no berries on the permanent mesh. Note the wish when berries are the species' read (rowan).
- **Budget.** Judge a run per metre, not against the crate-sized per-model prop median. The shipped StoneWallTumbled is 2 380 tris and the blind 4 m wall 2 388. Audit and score with **`--class run`**: efficiency is tris per metre (`--length`, or the longest horizontal size). The shipped median is **574 tris/m** (StoneWall 699, RaceWall 672, StoneWallTumbled 574, PathEdge 252, FenceRail 221), and the audit WARNs above 2x. The audit skips its density check on parts under its minimum surface area, so check a dense small part (a sapling) by its tri count.
