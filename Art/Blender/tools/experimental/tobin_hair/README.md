Decision: keep the approved hair_v5 C mesh (look retained) and add secondary motion with spring bone chains under the Head bone; DiffLocks strand output could not be trimmed into the reference style (see `difflocks_trim_experiment.py`, C:/AI/test_out/heroes/tobin/hair_v6/README.md).
- `hair_rig.py` (Blender, on `rig_v2/Tobin_rigged_v2.blend`): 44 k-means clumps of the hair mesh, each gets a 4-bone chain hair_<k>_0..3 under mixamorig:Head (bone 0 = non-deforming root); hair verts keep Head weight near the scalp (distance to the head mesh, smoothstep 0.6 -> 3.5 cm) and follow their chain below that; weights smoothed over the mesh so clump borders do not tear. Writes Tobin_rigged_hair.blend + chains.json.
- `sim_bake.py`: Blender preview of the same verlet chains with the Head bone yawing +-32 deg and nodding (renders a contact sheet; note the rig v2 blend has `pose_position = REST`, the script sets POSE).
- `export_fbx.py`: FBX with all 260 bones (rest pose).
- Unity: `Assets/_Project/Presentation/Characters/TobinHairSpringChains.cs` (put on the Tobin root; finds the Head bone and the hair_* bones by name, same solver as sim_bake: stiffness 1500, damping 0.93, gravity 9.8, 4 substeps; `weight` = 0 gives rigid hair). Presentation only, no authority.
Outputs: C:/AI/test_out/heroes/tobin/hair_v6/rig/{Tobin_rigged_hair.blend,.fbx,chains.json,sim_sheet.png}.

## Hair cleanup 2026-11-10 (black/silver chunks + strands through the head)
Root cause of the black/navy chunks: BACK faces of the strip sheets. Rendering with Cull=Front showed exactly those chunks (navy, lit as flipped back faces through gaps and at strip ends); Cull=Back showed clean hair. Fix in `hair_rig2.py`: every strip face oriented away from the head centre (5,494 of 16,778 faces flipped), material single-sided (`_Cull=2` in `TobinHairMaterialBuilder`), hair does not receive self-shadows (`TobinHairSpringChains.hairReceivesShadows=false`; hard self-shadow also darkened the sheets).
Strands through the head: `--clear 0.006` pushes every hair vertex inside the head surface (1,527 verts) or closer than 6 mm to 6 mm clearance (BVH on AvatarHead), cap offset 2.5 mm.
Debris removal (all parameters overridable as key=value after `--`): tiny faces (`tiny_a`), slivers (`shard_q`), near-black atlas samples (`dark_l`), flat strips lying on the eye/cheek area (`fz fx fd`), anything within 7 mm of the skin at the outer eye corner/temple, and small isolated pieces left behind (`iso iso_z`; 344 faces). Total about 2,100 + 344 faces removed. Trade-off: the sideburn/temple area is now bare in front of the ears.
Diagnostics kept: `flag.py` (candidate shard sets in red), capture env `SC_MATVAR=cullback|cullfront|nonorm|nomr`, `SC_NOSHAD=1`.
Reproduce: `blender -b --factory-startup -P hair_rig2.py -- <out>`, `blender -b <out>/Tobin_mp_hair.blend -P export_fbx2.py -- <out>/TobinHairMesh.fbx`, copy the FBX to Content/Characters/TobinBase/HairMesh, capture with `SC_REAL=1 SC_HQ=1 SC_SIZE=1400`.
Tiles: C:/AI/test_out/heroes/tobin/hair_v6/clean7/.

## Hair rebuilt as cards (2026-11-10, replaces the Tripo sheet mesh)
Why: the Tripo mesh could only be trimmed (a haircut, not the intended style). New route, all local:
1. Photoreal reference: FLUX.2 klein 9B edits the stylized concept head crop into a photoreal portrait with the same hairstyle (`v2/klein_views.py`, 10 s).
2. DiffLocks (WSL, `C:/AI/difflocks/run1.sh`, run with `MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1 wsl bash ...`) turns that portrait into ~100k strands: this time a side part, swept fringe, layered volume, sides over the ears (the stylized image gave a centre-parted bob).
3. `hair_trim.py` (difflocks_trim_experiment.py) fits the strands to the MetaPerson head and applies a mild length trim (fringe 1.735, sides 1.60, back 1.605, squeeze 0.95).
4. `cards/cards_from_strands.py`: clusters strands (K=260 by root + tip), one mean curve per cluster, 2 cards each (full + shorter, shifted), 9 rings, 3 verts across (bulged), tapered to a point, outward-facing, UV into an 8-variant strand atlas -> 520 cards, 14.6k tris (the old mesh: 16.8k ragged tris).
5. `cards/hair_atlas.py`: procedural strand atlas (RGBA colour with alpha-cut tips, normal, metal/smooth; env HA_N / HA_T0 / HA_T1 set strand count and thickness; used 70 strands, 7-13 px).
6. `hair_rig3.py`: same chain rig as hair_rig2 (`src=<cards.blend> fit=0 debris=0 shards=0 clear=0.005 K=48`; outward orientation, head clearance push, scalp cap over the whole crown `capall=1.77`), `export_fbx2.py`.
7. Unity: `TobinHairMaterialBuilder` now builds the card material (alpha clip 0.38, single-sided, mip coverage preserved); the capture (`SC_REAL=1 SC_HQ=1 SC_SIZE=1400`) renders it on TobinBase.
Known rough spots: the scalp cap shows as a flat brown patch at the parting and a faint band at the hairline; strand texture is procedural (stair-stepped chevrons where cards overlap on the crown); thin dark-blue wisps at the lowest side tips; fringe length over the eyes was set by eye. Tiles: C:/AI/test_out/heroes/tobin/hair_v7/cards6/.

## Hair rebuilt as a modelled shell + lock cards (2026-11-10, replaces the DiffLocks cards)
Why: the DiffLocks route "looks nothing like the reference". The hair is now authored to the reference (C:/AI/test_out/heroes/tobin/refs/crop_*.png) as a sheet that lies on the skull plus wispy lock cards; no DiffLocks or Tripo geometry is used. Scripts in `shell/`:
1. `hair_atlas2.py` (trellis venv python: numpy + cv2): strand textures drawn at 2x with anti-aliased curves, downsampled to 2048. `Hair_Cards_*` = 8 lock variants (RGBA, solid tapered lock body under the strands, pointed alpha tips, rounded-lock shading); `Hair_Shell_*` = opaque clumps tileable in u (4 locks per tile, soft dark gaps). Normal + metallic/smoothness maps from the strand height.
2. `build_shell_hair.py` (Blender 5.2): head = the project's **TobinBase.fbx** (the older Tobin_Base_v2_sliders.glb has a 2.4 cm smaller back of the skull / nape; a shell built on it sat inside the real head in Unity). Shell = lat/long grid with its pole at the crown whorl (meridians run the way hair grows), outermost ray-cast onto the head, radius field dilated + blurred (bridges the ears), thickness profile (thin fringe on the forehead, puff above the ears, crown volume, occiput bulge), sides taper in under the ears and into the nape, lower edge cut per signed world azimuth (`EDGE` table: diagonal fringe low on the character's right, shorter left of the part, temples, over the ears, V at the nape) with one pointed tooth per lock, lock bumps along swirled meridians (fringe sweep to the character's right, part groove a little to the character's left), smooth + BVH push (>= 4 mm from head, lashes and eyeballs). Closed slab (outer/inner/rim), inner+rim carry the outer normals. Cards = guide curves walking the shell flow, then leaving it with gravity / sweep / flick and clearance (head and shell): shingles over the whole shell with lifted tips, side locks (splay then turn in at the jaw), flared temple locks, nape flicks (longest at the centre), fringe locks walking diagonally across the forehead to the brow / eye line, short locks left of the part, crown wisps and a cowlick; each card has a reversed twin, both with the outward normal (the inward-lit twin was the black slivers on the crown). Per-vertex `free` group (0 on the shell, ramps to 1 within 3 cm past it) drives the rig. EEVEE previews `prev_*.png`; `sheet.sh <dir>` puts them under the reference crops.
3. `rig_export.py`: HairArmature = Head (from TobinBase's Head bone) + k-means chains over the free card vertices (17-18 chains x 4 bones), shell 100 % Head, exports `TobinHairMesh.fbx` (same frame/axes as export_fbx2.py, path_mode STRIP).
4. Unity: `TobinHairMaterialBuilder.BuildShell` (opaque URP Lit, `Tobin_HairShell`), `Apply` routes renderers named *HairShell* to it; capture `SC_FOV` env widens the frame.
Reproduce: `python shell/hair_atlas2.py <tex>`; `blender -b --factory-startup -P shell/build_shell_hair.py -- <out> tex=<tex>`; `blender -b <out>/Tobin_shell_hair.blend -P shell/rig_export.py -- <out>/TobinHairMesh.fbx`; copy the FBX and `Hair_Cards_*`/`Hair_Shell_*` PNGs into Content/Characters/TobinBase/HairMesh; capture with `SC_REAL=1 SC_HQ=1 SC_SIZE=1000 SC_FOV=11`.
Budget: shell 22.5k tris, cards ~24k tris (both sides), one 2K atlas per material.
