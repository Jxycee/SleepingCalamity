# CellarVault (B5 cellar vault kit)

Generated by `Art/Blender/cellars/sc_cellar_vault.py` (deterministic: seeds from zlib.crc32; two runs give identical tris):
`blender -b --factory-startup --python Art/Blender/cellars/sc_cellar_vault.py -- --out <scratch> --no-render --export --export-dir Assets/_Project/Content/Art/Cellar/B5/CellarVault`.
No unique textures: T2 kit on the shared tileables (`Art/Textures/B2_cellar`, `B1_town`: CC0 Poly Haven scans, see their SOURCES.md), metre UVs (2 m per tile).
All geometry, bottles, jars and roots are original procedural meshes. References (study only): `Reference/hollowmere/dungeon_corner/REFS.md`.

## Axes
Authored z up, exported -Z forward / Y up (bake_space_transform): Unity (x, y, z) = Blender (-x, z, -y); +Z runs along the vault. Identity transforms on every child.
Wall pieces face +Z (front at z = 0, body behind it). NicheWall is turned 180 degrees by the builder at z 8. LarderShelf is built on the left wall (x -2.5, extends to -2.05).
CoalChute is built on the right wall (x +2.5), centred z 0, hatch on the vault surface at about y 2.5. Barrels: lying barrels' axes run along X, their wall-side heads at local x -0.5 (place the origin about x -1.9 against the left wall); the standing barrel is at local z +1.5.

## Tileable parts and wear
Tileable-textured everything above except the flat/emissive slots. Wear: linear CORNER float colour `wear` (R grime/soot/damp, G moss, B polish). Written: damp tide line (dark wet courses
up to about 0.8 m with a pale salt bloom just above), moss at the wall foot, smoke up the vault, polish on stair treads, sills, shelf boards, grate bars, chute bed, iron; wine/damp at
the barrels' bilge. Lantern soot plumes and root stains exist only in the review room copies, not in this FBX. All solid pieces are closed meshes; single-sided quads are CV_Breath (faces up),
CV_Daylight (faces the room) and the crack ribbon in Roots (faces down).
