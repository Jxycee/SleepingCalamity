# Saltwake road props: sources and measurements (Q15)

All original geometry, generated by `Art/Blender/town/sc_saltwake_road.py` (pieces) and `Art/Blender/town/sc_road_trees.py` (far-tree atlas). No external models. Pieces use the shared
B1 tileables (slot name = Unity material name, metre UVs, linear `wear` colour attribute: R grime, G moss, B polish); hedges use the B9 foliage atlas (`B9_Foliage`) and carry a `Col` AO attribute too.
Run: `blender -b --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python Art/Blender/town/sc_saltwake_road.py -- --out <scratch> --export [--trees]`.

Axes for every piece (Blender, z up; FBX written -Z forward / Y up, FBX_SCALE_UNITS): pivot at the ground centre of the footprint, local ground z = 0 (parts buried 0.15-0.3 m below 0),
the side facing the road is -Y. Bounds below are the re-imported FBX in Blender axes (`sc_asset_audit.py`, 2026-10-31). The audit class `prop` flags "material slots > 2" on every multi-slot piece: that is by design (shared tileables, like the town wall), not a defect.

## Far-tree atlas (`FarTrees/`)

`T_Road_FarTrees_BaseColor.png` (2048 x 1024, sRGB, alpha = coverage, colour bled under transparent pixels) and `T_Road_FarTrees_Normal.png` (tangent-space crown normal seen from the front, R x right, G z up, B toward the viewer).
4 cells of 512 x 1024 px, cell k = columns 512k..512k+511; scale 64 px per metre (a cell is 8 m wide x 16 m tall); tree base at the cell's bottom centre (row 16 from the bottom, column 256 within the cell).
Round 2 (2026-10-31): four distinct storybook trees built from ragged lumpy leaf clumps (B9 leaf-mass cells for leaf-scale alpha and detail, per-clump dome normals, AO at each clump's lower edge and in the notches, top-left rim light, darker lower crown, 3 green hues from yellow-green tops to deep olive), over a dark interior layer, with visible trunks and limbs and a few sky gaps. Use as crossed billboard cards; scale 1 = the sizes below.
