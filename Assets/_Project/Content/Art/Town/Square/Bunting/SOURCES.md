# Bunting (Hollowmere square dressing)

Generated by `Art/Blender/town/sc_square_dressing.py` (see its header for the run line).

- Source: original, generated in the script (no external mesh). Textures are procedural; wood, iron, stone and cloth structure comes from the shared B1 tileables (`Art/Textures/B1_town`, their sources in its SOURCES.md).
- Licence: ours.
- Size (Blender x, y, z; Unity x, z, y): 1.15 x 0.06 x 0.39 m. Origin per child mesh (below); the parts are not laid out for placement: instantiate each by name. Authored z up, front -Y in Blender = +Z in Unity (Unity (x,y,z) = Blender (-x, z, -y)).
- 2732 tris, one material (all child meshes); maps 1024 px: `Bunting_BaseColor` (sRGB), `Bunting_Normal` (OpenGL, Y+), `Bunting_ORM` (R occlusion, G roughness, B metallic; linear).
- One FBX, one material, one texture set, SEVEN child meshes (all at the FBX origin; each mesh is built about its own pivot): `Pennant_0` (madder red), `Pennant_1` (ochre), `Pennant_2` (woad blue), `Pennant_3` (cream), `Pennant_T0` and `Pennant_T1` (torn, ragged, faded madder / woad), `Rope_1m`. The torn pennants live in this FBX (no separate Bunting_Torn folder).
- **Pennant**: origin = the middle of its top edge, where the rope passes. The top edge runs along local X, so **local X is "along the rope"** (it is Unity +X after export). The pennant **hangs down local -Y in Unity (Blender -Z)**; the cloth faces +/-Z in Unity (front = Unity +Z). About 0.30 m wide at the top, 0.38 m drop, hem folded over a 12 mm rope (hem inner radius 9 mm), solid 3 mm thick (two shells joined at the edges, so it is closed and double sided with no cull trouble).
- **Rope_1m**: origin at its start; it runs 1.00 m along **Unity +X** (authored along Blender -X; Unity x = -Blender x), 12 mm diameter, 8 sides, three-strand twist baked into the normal.
- Per-mesh bounds (m, Unity x/y/z): pennants 0.30 x 0.38 x ~0.03 (hem top at +0.0105 above the origin); rope 1.00 x 0.012 x 0.012.
