# Hollowmere trees (TR01-TR07): sources and licence

All seven are Poly Haven models, licence CC0 (https://polyhaven.com/license). Downloaded 2026-11-16 (2K glTF), adopted with `Art/Blender/town/sc_trees_adopt.py`
(leaf islands replaced by alpha cards, LODs, crown normals, textures repacked: albedo(+alpha) / normal / ms (A = 1 - roughness) / occ). Raw downloads are not
kept in the repo; re-fetch by id with `Art/Blender/tools/sc_fetch_models.py`.

| code | Poly Haven id | URL | licence | role | final height | changes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TR01 | tree_small_02 | https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_small_02 | CC0 | small broadleaf (lot trees) | 6 m | 4.7 M tris reduced; trunk atlas as bark, branches box-UV'd onto it |
| TR02 | island_tree_01 | https://polyhaven.com/a/island_tree_01 | CC0 | big broadleaf (square, churchyard) | 13 m | as above |
| TR03 | island_tree_02 | https://polyhaven.com/a/island_tree_02 | CC0 | medium broadleaf (belt, hill forest) | 9 m | as above |
| TR04 | fir_sapling_medium | https://polyhaven.com/a/fir_sapling_medium | CC0 | conifer (hill forest) | 11 m | variant "a" only; branches rebuilt as low-poly tapered tubes |
| TR05 | shrub_04 (plant cluster 1 of 4) | https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_04 | CC0 | tall shrub / sapling | 2.2 m | one of the four plants in the file; 1K |
| TR06 | shrub_04 (plant cluster 2 of 4) | https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_04 | CC0 | low shrub | 0.9 m | another plant of the same file; 1K |
| TR07 | shrub_04 (plant cluster 3 of 4) | https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_04 | CC0 | low bush | 0.7 m | a third plant of the same file; 1K |

Substitution: shrub_02, shrub_03 (the brief's TR05/TR06 sources) were sparse, a flat plank-like plant and unusable after reduction; shrub_04 holds four separate small plants, so TR05-TR07 are three of them.
