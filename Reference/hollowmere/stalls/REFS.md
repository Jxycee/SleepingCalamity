# hollowmere/stalls

Use: the three fixed market stalls of the Hollowmere square (`Art/Blender/town/sc_market_stall.py --variant produce|cloth|tools --state ruined|rebuilt`): goods per trade, how a stall decays.

Study material only: never shipped, traced or projected (sc-reference). Found through Openverse (`Art/Blender/tools/sc_ref_fetch.py`) on 2026-10-31; licence from the Openverse record. Gate: CC0/PD/CC BY only. Resized to 1024 px on the long side. The produce stall's reference is the existing `Reference/hollowmere/props/bc_312_stratford_upon_avon_rother_market_.jpg`.
