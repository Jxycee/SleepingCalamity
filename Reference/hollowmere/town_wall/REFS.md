# hollowmere/town_wall

Use: the town wall kit and the Lower Gate and East Gate (`Art/Blender/town/sc_town_wall.py`, queue item Q8): a weathered sandstone curtain wall with a walkway and crenels, its ruined and patched variants, and two gatehouses with round arches and timber doors.

Study material only: never shipped, traced or projected (sc-reference). Gate: CC0, public domain or CC BY only (CC BY-SA is excluded by the project gate). Wikimedia Commons rate-limited the search API on 2026-10-31 (HTTP 429, "too many requests"), so as the brief allows this folder reuses the licence-checked repo references instead of fetching new ones. No images are copied here (they stay in their own folders, which hold the files and the licence record); the table lists where each lives, its licence as recorded there, and what we take from it. The CC BY credit is the creator column.
