## Findings, ranked

1. **F1, should-fix: the overlay heal count can double.** `Core/Diagnostics/DiveStatsModel.cs:59-61`. One heal counts when loaves drop and another when HP rises with loaves unchanged. On a client, `NetHp` and `NetLoaves` arrive in different frames, so one loaf counts as 2 in either order. The test at `DiveStatsModelTests.cs:19-29` only covers the same-frame case. **Being fixed** on `claude/overlay-heal-count`: an HP rise and a loaf drop within a short window count as one heal.
2. **F2, should-fix (medium): overlay stats reset mid-dive.** `Presentation/Cellars/DiveStatsOverlay.cs:63`. `active` requires the owned hero to be present, so one frame without a hero (respawn, reconnect) zeroes the dive. **Being fixed** on the same branch: `active` comes from the director's `NetActive`.
3. **F3, low: `CoopDiveFastTests` can pass vacuously.** `Tests/PlayMode/Bot/CoopDiveFastTests.cs:36-49`. It asserts neither `run.MaxFloor >= 1` nor `run.Seconds >= 90`, and `CoopDiveRun.Play` (`CoopDiveRun.cs:149`) breaks silently at its real-time cap. A host bot that never leaves the hub still passes. Fix: add both asserts. Parked.
4. **F4, low: possible one-frame unheld fall at a stair swap on clients.** `Presentation/Cellars/DiveDirector.cs:518-520` (`UpdateHold`). The hold is keyed on `HaulSync.CurrentFloor`, but the client rebuilds when `FloorSync.Net` lands, and those are two NetworkVariables on different objects. If FloorSync arrives first, the old floor is gone while the hold still reads the applied floor. Rare; the netsim test passes. Fix: key the hold on the floor FloorSync is showing, with HaulSync as the fallback. **Routed** by the coordinator to the dive-flow session's stair follow-up branch.
5. **F5, low: room clear times lost at a floor change.** `DiveStatsModel.cs:64,80`. `StartFloor` clears `_roomEntered` after the host's first `RoomEntered` may already have landed. Revisiting a floor logs its existing cleared bits as fresh clears. Fix: key by (floor, room) and treat the first nonzero mask as the baseline. Parked (dev overlay).
6. **F6, low (unconfirmed): `ConnectedClientsIds` read on a client.** `DiveStatsOverlay.cs:61`. Older NGO versions throw `NotServerException` here. **Guarded** on `claude/overlay-heal-count`.
7. **F7, low: test cleanup only on the success path.** `Tests/PlayMode/StairHandoverTests.cs:191-206`. `TestHubGround` is destroyed only when the test succeeds. Fix: destroy it in `[UnityTearDown]`. Parked.
8. **Doc: `docs/design/m7-tuning-notes.md`** still listed the hunter and rat-kings as tier 2 and tier 1 as "floors 1-2". After `62175e68` only the Warden is tier 2 by default. **Fixed** in the same commit as this file.

Traced by hand and fine, but not covered by tests: a client dropping mid-hold (the hold is released on despawn), a late join while a floor is pending (the 0.2 s placement pass teleports it), and the 10 s hold cap (the timed-out teleport still lands).
