## Method and limits

- NavMesh: baked from physics colliders (triggers left out) with an agent of the hero's radius, height, step offset and slope limit (voxel 0.1 m), so reachability is "walk there and back". Jumps don't count for reachability; for stuck pockets the hero escapes if the climb is at most step offset + jump height.
- Narrow: the capsule plus half the comfort margin doesn't fit, and the free width from thin capsule casts along four axes is below diameter + margin. Blocked: the bare capsule doesn't fit on the line ("widest fit" is the largest capsule that does).
- Walk-through and invisible checks compare the colliders against the visible meshes (temporary MeshColliders on a spare layer). Soft dressing (foliage, ivy, cloth, lights, hanging signs, lanterns, water, decals) is skipped on purpose.
- Hollowmere has no player spawn yet (M6 S3 adds the dive hub), so its largest walkable area counts as the main area. Doors of rebuilt buildings are closed boxes: a door counts as reached when the hero can stand within 1.5 m of it.
- Likely source: the first builder line that names the object (searched in Editor/Builders, Presentation/Cellars, Core/Cellars, Core/Town). Treat it as a pointer, not proof.
