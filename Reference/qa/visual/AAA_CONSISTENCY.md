# Hollowmere AAA consistency list

The starting-area cohesion target is in `docs/PHASE1.md` section 3: NieR Replicant's opening village as a quality and cohesion reference, with our own look kept. This list comes from a code and data scan, because Hollowmere captures crash in the cloud. Items marked **GPU** need eyes on a capture from a local PC session. Stairs and traversal are out of scope here (other sessions own them).

Started 2026-11-02 by Cloud Tasker (scan of main `ae76bf4`). Format: item, where, why, suggested fix, owner/state.
