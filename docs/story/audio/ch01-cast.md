## Notes on who is who

- TOBIN and HOB share a rural timbre on purpose (father and son) but keep Hob much lower and slower.
- ODILE, JORY and ISOLDE speak only in lines recalled inside narration (the packing of the buckler, the handing over of the bow, the kiss and the staff). If you do not want extra voices for 5 short lines, give them to HOB (for JORY use ASH's voice raised) or leave them to the NARRATOR with the line read in a different tone. The script labels them separately so you can choose.
- No other characters speak in chapter 1. Amos Gill, Abner Stroud, Marta Fenn, Dun Harrow, the Sedge boy and Constable Pell are named but silent; no UNNAMED KIDS lines.
- Scene 1.13 has a remembered italic line (Hob in Tobin's memory, "Stop pushing it..."); it is given to HOB with a `[warmly]` tag. The italic thought "It's not been done right." near the end of 1.15 is left with NARRATOR (it is Tobin's thought, quoting Hollis).

## How to use

1. Read this table and choose one library voice per label. Keep the same voice for the same label in every scene and every chapter.
2. Paste one scene at a time from `ch01-script.md` as its own generation (each `### 1.N` section, without the `(SCENE: ...)` note), so a bad take costs one scene, not the chapter.
3. In ElevenLabs Studio / Projects, add each `SPEAKER: text` line as a separate paragraph and assign that paragraph the speaker's voice. Or use the Text-to-Dialogue feature (v3): feed the labelled lines with one voice per label. Audio tags such as `[quietly]` work in v3; delete them if you use a model that does not support tags.
4. Try `ch01-free-tier-test.md` (scene 1.1 only) first to check the voices, then do the rest.
5. Free tier: 10,000 credits per month is roughly 10,000 characters of text. The test excerpt is under half of that. The full chapter is about 60,000 characters, so it needs a paid plan or several months of free tier.
