## Known canon notes (chapter writers' fixes to Part A, to fold into the bible)
- Freed sleepers are breathed up a shaft to their own plot (not through trapdoor light); the shaft closes behind them.
- Sigils on sealed alcoves are town marks, not script letters.
- Unticked names at the end of the Undercroft: 28 (children were never in it), not 3.
- Tobin's first spoken lies are earlier than Part A says (chapter 1).
- Ch2 slip to fix on annotation pass: Pip banks her boots (2.4) but wears "her new boots" on floor 7 (2.6).
- Ch3: Saltwake's confession comes after the boss (Part B had it before); people-left line is glass net-floats.
- Chapter numbering: the bible's "ch3" (Cinderholt) is book chapter 4, because the book gives Hollowmere two chapters. Pins 12-13 ("ch1-3", "ch4") are read in bible numbering.
- Ch4: Ash learns he is Elder Vane's great-grandson here, through the Old Seam's ward word "Bettany" (Vane's daughter, Hester's mother, died PY 40, before Ash was born). He doesn't learn that Vane is alive or is the steward; the orchard scene (B10) keeps both.
- Ch4: the first floor-8 visit ends in a failed dive on floor 9, so the trade falls between dives. Jory, Dagny and the Engine come in one later dive, so the steward speaks before Tobin's punch (Part B had it after).
- Ch4: Jory's warm rock is the scree fissure (he covered it with a cairn), not a spot in the founders' tunnels. Cinderholt's furnaces survive the wipe because they're cut into the cliff. People-left line is pit checks. Dagny's end-of-shift whistle is new. Ebben's engine rises home with him.
- Ch5: Wren's staff forms 1-2 (*Plumb Line*, Isolde laying out Thistledown's orchard; *The Collar*) fired off-page on the Arcade's floor 9 and the Glow Seams' floor 7, and Wren hid them. *The Vote* (Hive floor 7) is how she first sees her mother's hand go up; the last memory matches ch1 1.4 exactly; True North on floor 8.
- Ch5: the script's cost: every written mark takes warmth from the writing hand, and a big one turns it to stone. Isolde's right forefinger greyed a little with each working (the step, the collar, the staff's foot) and the church ward took it to the second knuckle; Wren's goes grey to the first knuckle freeing Tobin on floor 10.
- Ch5: Isolde studied the old tally pages in Thistledown's abbey scriptorium every spring for eleven years, which is why the dream sets her there. Her page lists the conspirators by initials (O.O., H.M., J.C., I.H.) with their children, the vote "carried five to two", and a script line burned off after "whoever stands at the table unbound". The clause stays unread until the Orrery dome.
- Ch5: the orchard reveal builds on ch4. Vane is alive, the signer and the steward, and his voice is the one from beside the pit. He tells Ash the ward word was on the parish roll and that Amos or Odile would have given it free. The "bait" turn: the hook is the page Wren carries home.
- Ch5: Lisle served "for everyone at the table" and asked nothing for himself (reconciles Part A 4.3 with Part B). The Queen is Mother Bounty, the abbey's gilt procession figure. The choir novices are adults and come home; children stay at the table. The people-left line is a capped comb frame (one bee out per rescue). Hep Lowe's grandson Dickon is at the table.
- Ch5: seeds of Isolde and Hob, with neither Tobin nor Wren able to read them: Hep's "big fellow" who carried the ledger-keeper's books, Isolde's "He would", and the desk Hob builds unasked.
- Ch6 order: the fracture (Pip finds the page, the silencing, the empty meeting, Tobin's confession) comes mid-chapter. It falls after Orrery floors 1-5, the night attack and the Provost's confession, and before floor 6, which means two trips home (two days each way). Part B put it before the climb. Pip's "one night" starts when Tuppence finds the page.
- Ch6, where the clause hid: the forfeit form came up from below the first autumn. Every ledger-keeper copied its last line in the script at the foot of every tally page, by eye, unable to read it. Isolde read it there. Vane put his hand over it when Wyck copied the contract (the clerk's note on Orrery floor 8: "Here the Elder of Hollowmere put his hand on the page and told me I was done"). He also set the candles so it burned off Isolde's page. The full text is read only at the Orrery dome.
- Ch6, the dome text: rich fields; a tithe that grows with every feeding; "Whoever eats at the feast is the table, and is held from that meal until the next"; weighed after the meal; "If the tithe falls short, the table is forfeit."; "What is owed will be taken, and what is given will be had, and nothing else"; the signer keeps the table, his heart held against it, given back "when the last table is closed, and he may sleep"; "Whoever stands at the table unbound, awake before the Host, may speak against this writing, and what is spoken will end it." Rescued townsfolk are awake but still bound (they ate in PY 50 and no feast has been held since), so only the four are unbound.
- Ch6, Stillhollow: stone stores at the head of the pass with 206 pack-saddles chalked with every name (the Provost's says only "Provost"). The town meant to leave in spring PY 51 (Sorrel Kett was paid in advance). Each autumn the Provost flashed the lens signal (three long, one short) to tell the steward the tithe was on the road, and an unsigned letter came back up in the empty carts: "The account is in order. Thank you for your light." Prudence copied them; Vane's early signed letters hold the famine. Edda's lens is the first piece out of the Orrery. People-left line: a celestial globe, one star out per rescue.
- Ch6, the night attack: Gleaners knock on every door with plates and take no one. They break only the door where something is owed (the four's lodge) and come for Wren. Stroud whistles them home. The Provost's sum: filling the four seats closes Hollowmere's account, "four against six towns". The town turns its back on him.
- Ch6, Isolde: she is packing for the pass road. She refuses to say the clause ("if I tell you, I put you there"). She tells Wren the letters end when the demon ends (Part B 3.3 had this in ch4). Wren keeps that one secret and is proud of it; everything else goes on the table.
- Ch6 carried marks: the silencing (three breaths) takes Wren's right forefinger to grey at the second knuckle, matching Isolde's. On the empty night Ash unstacks Jory's cairn, and the voice says nothing all night.
- Ch6, Vane's hold: the Orrery turns around him at a still centre. His commands work only on the bound, so his word slides off Ash. Stroud dies by one spoken word. Four hands push Vane off his stone; the Orrery stops and the stars become the contract, readable by anyone. After that Vane can't command, and the Orrery's Gleaners lay down their plates and sickles and walk down toward the Hearth.
- Ch6, Hester: found beside two worn hollows where Vane stood for ten years (Ash's feet fit them). She goes home to Hollowmere, and at the top of the lake road she walks past Vane without a word. Vane is held in the watch-house with Odile on the door. Wyck leaves the pass charts on the dome step. Stroud is painted out in ch6, and Marta nods.
