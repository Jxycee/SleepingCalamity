## Known canon notes (chapter writers' fixes to Part A, to fold into the bible)
- Freed sleepers are breathed up a shaft to their own plot (not through trapdoor light); the shaft closes behind them.
- Sigils on sealed alcoves are town marks, not script letters.
- Unticked names at the end of the Undercroft: 28 (children were never in it), not 3.
- Tobin's first spoken lies are earlier than Part A says (chapter 1).
- Ch2 slip to fix on annotation pass: Pip banks her boots (2.4) but wears "her new boots" on floor 7 (2.6).
- Ch3: Saltwake's confession comes after the boss (Part B had it before); people-left line is glass net-floats.
- Chapter numbering: the bible's "ch3" (Cinderholt) is book chapter 4, because the book gives Hollowmere two chapters. Pins 12-13 ("ch1-3", "ch4") are read in bible numbering.
- Ch4: Ash learns he is Elder Vane's great-grandson here, through the Old Seam's ward word "Bettany" (Vane's daughter, Hester's mother, died PY 40, before Ash was born). He doesn't learn that Vane is alive or is the steward; the orchard scene (B10) keeps both.
- Ch4: the first floor-8 visit ends in a failed dive on floor 9, so the trade falls between dives. Jory, Dagny and the Engine come in one later dive, so the steward speaks before Tobin's punch (Part B had it after).
- Ch4: Jory's warm rock is the scree fissure (he covered it with a cairn), not a spot in the founders' tunnels. Cinderholt's furnaces survive the wipe because they're cut into the cliff. People-left line is pit checks. Dagny's end-of-shift whistle is new. Ebben's engine rises home with him.
