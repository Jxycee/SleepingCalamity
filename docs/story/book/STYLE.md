# Book style guide (for chapter writers)

Draft for the user's sign-off, annotated by the user. Not canon until signed off. Source layers: docs/superpowers/specs/story-bible-part-a.md (canon pins) and part-b.

- Each chapter file: `NN-title.md`. First lines: `# Chapter N: Title`, then `*Status: draft, for user annotation.*`, then scenes separated by `---`. Each scene starts with an italic one-line place/time tag. Number each scene `### N.M` so the user can comment by reference. End each chapter with a short `**Game hooks:**` list (what in-game scenes, pieces, people, stir beats this chapter feeds) and `**Open for the user:**` list (up to 5 questions).
- Voice: close third person, rotating hero per scene (one POV per scene). Concrete, dry, funny when people are scared, cruel when people are scared. Morally grey: everyone, heroes included, does one selfish or ugly thing with reasons. Violence is quick, costs something, leaves marks that carry forward. Gallows humour. No sex, no gratuitous gore. Storybook surroundings (saturated day colour); dread lives in people and choices.
- IP: tone only. No names, plots, scenes or text from Dark Cloud or Abercrombie, no imitation of his prose. Own words.
- Canon: obey the canon pins; never contradict STORY.md settled text; placeholder names (Tuppence, Hollis Rye, Marta Fenn) stay until renamed.
- Length: free; aim 6-10k words a chapter; prose first, dialogue-rich.
