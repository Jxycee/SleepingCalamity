# Cellars Room Pool on Room Data v2: Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Give every Cellars pool room (12 combat, 3 specials, 6 corridors) and the 6 bakery set-piece rooms room data v2 that passes `RoomValidatorV2`, and grow the combat rooms that can't hold a full co-op fight, so plan 3 builds its floors on v2 rooms.

**Architecture:** A new `Core/Rooms/CellarRoomsV2.cs` wraps the pool's and the bakery's `CellarRoom` objects **by reference** (`RoomDataV2.FromV1`), so there is one source of geometry: the generator keeps reading v1, plan 3 reads v2, and both see the same rooms. Six undersized combat rooms are resized in place in `CellarRoomPool.cs` (one row or column, or one solid cell opened), which deliberately moves the seed-12345 floor 1-3 goldens. The one room left small (the stoke chicane) and the bakery rooms are listed with a reason and a party size they do fit.

**Tech Stack:** C# 9 (Unity 6000.6.5f1), NUnit 3, `python Tools/cloud/sc_core_tests.py` for the inner loop, `Tools/unity.ps1 gate|check|soak`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-hearthdeep-dungeon-design.md` sections 3.2 (gimmicks and tools), 5 (the Undercroft floors: the 12 rooms return on floors 2, 4 and 6-7), 6 (floor recipe), 7 (rooms and encounters: at most 8 live enemies with four players), 11.5 (F7: room data v2 before room 13). Roadmap: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-03-dive-build-roadmap.md`. Room rules: `docs/ROOM_METRICS.md`. Model: plan 4 (`2026-11-03-metrics-gym.md`), `PilotRoomsV2.cs`.

## Decisions this plan makes
- **The generator stays on v1 until plan 3.** Plan 3 rewrites floor generation for the 7-floor dive and owns the door seam rule and the flood-move rule, so switching the reader now would be rewritten anyway and would move every golden twice. The risk worth closing now is drift between v1 and v2, so `CellarRoomsV2` wraps the very same `CellarRoom` objects (`ReferenceEquals`, pinned by a test). The soak then covers exactly the geometry plan 3 will read.
- **Resize six rooms, the least that fits:** pantry 2x3 to 3x5, cold store 3x4 to 3x5, cask row 4x3 to 5x3, bunker 3x3 to 3x5 (one row or column each, the pantry two of each), L vault and furnace ring open one solid cell inside their 4x4. Ids, names, themes, silhouettes, door counts, spawn counts, chest and key marks stay; sockets move only where a new edge needs them. Grids are in Task 2.
- **The stoke chicane stays 2x5, a small-party room.** Its zigzag is the room's idea and a third column erases it; it fits two heroes and its own two spawns (`Fits(room, 2, 2)`). Plan 3's encounters must not put more than its capacity in it. The coal theme keeps four full-party rooms (bunker, ring, boiler hall) plus this one.
- **Bakery rooms get v2 data but no resize.** They are a set piece with a designed lighter guard (spec 5, floor 3 "a lighter guard than a boss"), plan 3 reuses them only as the placeholder floor-3 set piece, and resizing would move the floor-4 goldens. Each one fits four heroes and its own authored spawns (`Fits(room, 4, spawns)`), which is recorded as its reason.
- **v2 extras stay empty on purpose.** Levels all 0, no gimmick regions, no stairs, no tool sockets, doors as `FromV1` types (`Locked` on the `L` door, else `Plain`). The spec ties tool sockets to valves, sluices and dampers (3.2), and balconies to the gimmick's dry state, all of which are the water/heat layer (spec 12 step 5), out of scope here. No wide arches: nothing in the spec asks for one and no builder reads v2 doors yet.
- **Corridors are v2 in canonical orientation only.** The generator rotates them; v2 has no rotation, which is harmless while levels and extras are empty. Plan 3 adds rotation if it ever gives a corridor a level or a typed door.
- **`PilotRoomsV2.HangingHall` stays a separate sample.** Its base room differs from the pool's hanging hall (a pillar and a chest moved); the v2 set wraps the pool's copy.
- **Habitat:** no authored tags (every room has the same 5.1 m vault, so `HighCeiling` would mean nothing). The computed tags are pinned per room by a test and the Dough Warden's habitat (`OpenFloor`) is checked wherever a spawn can roll it.

## Review Focus
1. A locked floor with a 4-player party: the lockable and key rooms it must draw are full-party rooms after the resize (Task 1 test `EveryCombatRoom_FitsAFullParty_OrIsListedSmall`).
2. v1/v2 drift: a later edit to the pool that forgets the v2 set, or a v2 copy of a room (Task 1 test `Pool_IsWrappedByReference`).
3. A stale small-room entry left after a room is grown (Task 1 test `SmallPartyList_HasNoRoomThatFits`).
4. A Tier2 spawn rolling an enemy that needs open floor in a room without a 3x3 open window (Task 1 test `EverySpawn_CanHostTheKindsItRolls`).
5. Bigger rooms starving the generator: more rejected plans or fallback floors (Task 2 footprint measurement and the soak in Task 4).
